Music

I make chiprock music, taking SID sounds and rock and seeing what cool sounds I can make with them. I am heavily influenced by Kubbi, Anamanaguchi, and the J.Arthur Keenes Band, as well as bands like Megadeth, T-SQUARE, and Buckethead. Have a listen and let me know what you think!

No geneative AI is used in any part of the song writing process.
